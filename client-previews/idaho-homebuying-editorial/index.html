How Idaho’s $125,000 Homeowner Tax Exemption Cuts Your Monthly Payment

Many first-time buyers calculate property taxes using out-of-state rules. In Idaho, primary residences qualify for an automatic 50% exemption up to $125,000 of assessed value—saving a typical Boise homeowner $1,140 per year ($95/month) from day one.

💡 Key Data Takeaway for 2026 Buyers
Filing takes 3 minutes online with your County Assessor after closing. There is no occupancy waiting period—file before December 31 to lock in your full reduction.
