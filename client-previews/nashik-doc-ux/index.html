UX Interaction Architecture
How We Solved "Watch, Pause, Read & Resume" on Mobile
1. The Documentary Stays the Spine When a user taps an era (Satavahana) or an evidence chip (Inscriptions), the video auto-pauses and docks at the top instead of navigating to a new page. Zero disorientation.
2. Synced Narration Cues (Tap-to-Dig-Deeper) As the documentary narrates an empire, monument, or coin hoard, a soft 4-second toast appears inside the player (💡 Pandavleni Caves • ⏸ Pause & Read). One thumb tap pauses the video and opens that exact card.
3. Sticky Return Anchor ("Resume at 04:18") While reading deep inside Places, People, or Primary sources, a high-contrast ▶ Resume Film (04:18) bar stays pinned below the video. Even if the user swipes across eras (Medieval → Maratha), their original 04:18 video timestamp is preserved!
4. "Bookmark for Later" (Zero-Interruption Mode) If a viewer doesn’t want to pause mid-scene, tapping 🔖 on any cue saves that historical card to the bottom Saved tab for post-film reading.
