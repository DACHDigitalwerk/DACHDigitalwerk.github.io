Our Story & Mission

A Boutique Approach to Thoroughbred Racing.

We founded Vanguard Racing with a single conviction: racehorse syndication should feel personal, disciplined, and exhilarating—never overcrowded or opaque.

The Syndicate Philosophy

Small Ownership Groups. Uncompromised Standards.

Traditional racing syndicates often dilute the ownership experience across hundreds of micro-holders. We take the opposite path: keeping each horse’s syndicate intentionally compact so every partner enjoys genuine connection with the stable, the trainer, and fellow co-owners.

Our mission is to pair patient, selective bloodstock acquisition with modern communication—delivering crisp video updates, transparent cost management, and unforgettable race-day hospitality.
